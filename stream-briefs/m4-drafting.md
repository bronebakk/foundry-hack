## Your goal
A keyworker requests a **case-note draft** for a meeting just held, and a **follow-up
message** to the young person. Both arrive as **editable drafts the worker authors and
commits** — in the worker's own voice. **Nothing is committed or sent autonomously.** This
is the most-watched surface for the core demo primitive: a judge must see "AI proposes,
human disposes" here in seconds.

## Your assertions (definition of done)
- **VAL-DRAFT-001** — Requesting a case note produces a draft in an editable field, clearly
  marked as an uncommitted draft (not yet part of the record of truth).
- **VAL-DRAFT-002** *(anchors Invariant 5 — authorship)* — The worker edits the draft, then
  commits; the committed record reflects the worker's edits and is **attributed to the
  worker, not the AI**. Evidence: committed record showing edits + worker authorship.
- **VAL-PROPOSE-001** — After generation with no human click, nothing is persisted (the
  committed view shows no new record). Generation alone changes no committed state.
- **VAL-PROPOSE-002** — The follow-up message stays a draft; sending requires a separate,
  explicit human send action. The outbound log shows no send until the worker clicked send.
- **VAL-PROPOSE-003** — Every AI output is framed as a proposal the worker can accept, edit,
  or **discard** — never as a settled decision. (Use `_proposal.html`.)

## What to build
1. **`GET /drafting/{persona_id}`** (and a way to reach it from the persona/caseload) with
   two actions:
   - **Draft a case note** — `provider.complete(...)` produces a note from the persona's
     context (and optionally a short worker "what happened in the meeting" input). Render it
     via `_proposal.html` as an **editable** draft with actions
     `[{"value":"commit","label":"Commit as my note"}, {"value":"discard","label":"Discard","class":"secondary outline"}]`.
   - **Draft a follow-up message** to the young person — same pattern, `ProposalType.FOLLOW_UP`,
     with actions `[{"value":"send","label":"Send message"}, {"value":"discard","label":"Discard","class":"secondary outline"}]`.
2. **`POST` disposition endpoints** (one per action, or one shared) that:
   - Reconstruct the `Proposal` from the posted hidden fields (`persona_id`, `surface`,
     `proposal_type`, `proposal_text`, `model`) and take `final_text` from the textarea.
   - Call `decision_log.record(proposal, Disposition.COMMIT/SEND/DISCARD,
     author=config.DEMO_WORKER, final_text=...)`. **Author is server-side (D-004) — never
     from the form.**
   - On **commit**: show the committed note attributed to the worker (e.g. "Committed by
     {{ worker }}" — never "generated by AI").
   - On **send**: only here does the message count as sent; reflect it in an **outbound/sent
     log** view you render (so VAL-PROPOSE-002 is demonstrable: nothing in the sent log until
     the explicit send). A real email/SMS gateway is out of scope (see missions.md) — the
     "send" is an in-app action + a sent-log entry.
   - On **discard**: log the discard (no `final_text`) and show nothing was kept.
3. **A "committed notes" / "sent messages" view** for the persona so a reviewer can confirm
   that *generation produced nothing here* until an explicit action.

## Voice & authorship
The worker's voice matters (it's their professional identity, and pride is a feature). Let
the draft be fully editable, commit the worker's edited text verbatim as `final_text`, and
attribute it to the worker. The original AI proposal is preserved separately in the log
(`proposal_text`) for the audit trail — but the *record of truth* is the worker's version.

## Inference / offline
- Open-weight defaults only. If `provider.configured` is False, render a clear "inference
  not configured" state (no 500); you can still demonstrate the boundary with a canned draft
  if you want, but mark it clearly.

## Tests to add (`tests/test_m4_drafting.py`)
- **VAL-PROPOSE-001 at the HTTP layer:** generating/viewing a draft creates **no**
  decision-log entry; only the POST does. Assert `decision_log.list_entries()` count is
  unchanged after a GET/generate and increments by exactly one after a commit.
- **VAL-DRAFT-002:** after a commit, the entry's `author == config.DEMO_WORKER` and
  `final_text` equals the (edited) submitted text, while `proposal_text` holds the original.
- **VAL-PROPOSE-002:** no "sent" entry exists until the send POST; a send creates a
  `Disposition.SEND` entry.
- **Authorship can't be spoofed:** posting an `author` form field is ignored — the recorded
  author is still `config.DEMO_WORKER`.
- Keep all existing tests green.

## Don'ts
- No write on generation. No autonomous send. Don't trust a client-supplied author.
- Don't edit frozen files. Don't merge to `main`.
